## How Common Is Same-Sex Divorce?

According to a 2013 survey by CBS, the average divorce rate among couples is about 36.3%, regardless of their sexuality. Among gay men, it’s believed that the rate of separation is closer to 15%, while lesbian couples are expected to separate about 30% of the time.

Though LGBTQ+ spouses seem less likely to divorce than their heterosexual counterparts, it’s always best to be prepared for the possibility.

### Children in Same-Sex Divorce Matters

In addition to the financial issues inherent in any divorce matter, LGBTQ+ couples face additional challenges when it comes to the rights and obligations of their minor children.

Texas law doesn’t recognize the presumption that a non-biological parent is a legal parent. And challenges raised in custody matters for heterosexual parents, such as DNA testing, may not apply to a same-sex couple. Divorcing LGBTQ+ couples must then contend with issues where one parent may be denied custody or visitation, or a biological parent is left without the means to support their child, should the breadwinner of the family decide to leave.

Additional complications arise when a child or children were adopted.

It is important to work with a law firm that will not only stand up for your right as a parent but can do so while navigating the complex emotional and legal matters surrounding custody, visitation, and child support matters in same-sex divorces.
