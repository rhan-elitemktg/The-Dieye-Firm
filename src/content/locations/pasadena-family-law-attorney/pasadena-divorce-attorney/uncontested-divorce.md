## How Uncontested Divorce Works in Pasadena, TX

Understanding the process can help you feel more confident about your decision. In Texas, an uncontested divorce requires that both spouses agree on all essential matters, such as property division, debts, [custody](/pasadena-family-law-attorney/child-custody/), and [child support](/pasadena-child-support-attorney/). This agreement allows you to move through the legal process more quickly, with less cost and less exposure to conflict in Harris County courts.

### Eligibility & Local Requirements

To file for an uncontested divorce in Pasadena, you or your spouse must have lived in Texas for at least six months and in Harris County for a minimum of 90 days. You will both need to agree fully on the settlement terms before the process moves forward. Once documents are prepared and filed with the Harris County District Clerk, Texas law generally requires a 60-day waiting period before the court will sign the final decree. Most uncontested divorces in Pasadena are finalized without a courtroom appearance, unless the court asks for more information.
