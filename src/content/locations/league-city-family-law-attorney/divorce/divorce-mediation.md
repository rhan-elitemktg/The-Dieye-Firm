## How Divorce Mediation Works in League City

Divorce mediation gives both spouses the chance to craft their own solutions, guided by a neutral third-party mediator. In League City and throughout Texas, mediation can address issues like child custody, property division, and support—all outside of a public courtroom. Texas law encourages couples to try mediation before proceeding to litigation because the process often leads to more personalized, mutually agreed outcomes. Court orders reached through mediation carry the same legal strength as those ordered by a judge—but you and your spouse hold more control over the decisions.

## What to Expect from the Mediation Process in League City

Your involvement in mediation usually begins with an orientation session where both parties outline their main concerns. Following this, each session highlights priorities and explores potential middle ground. Our team prepares you for each step, provides knowledgeable guidance, and works with you to shape solutions on issues such as parenting, property, and support. We ensure every agreement aligns with Texas law and your goals.

- Sessions occur in private conference rooms or virtually, maintaining confidentiality and convenience.
- Mediators remain neutral, helping both sides communicate and negotiate without taking sides or making decisions for you.
- Agreements reached in mediation, after your legal review, can be submitted to the court for approval just like a traditional settlement.
- If you cannot resolve all issues in mediation, you may choose to bring the remaining matters before a judge.
